5. Risk management system & FRIA

FRIA 33d6f8b7-78a9-41c7-bb18-37fac6d31d2c · Status APPROVED · Deployer Hellenic Atlantic Bank S.A.

Risks identified

RiskLikelihoodSeverityAffected rights
Disparate impact on younger applicants (<25) due to thin-file bias. MEDIUM MEDIUM Non-discrimination, Right to economic participation
Decline-explainability gap when ensemble disagreement is high. MEDIUM HIGH Right to explanation under GDPR Art. 22
Cross-border data transfer in event of branch failover. LOW MEDIUM Data protection

Mitigations

  • Shadow-mode review of all decline decisions for under-25 applicants by a credit officer for the first 90 days post-deployment. — owner: Head of Consumer Credit, review: Weekly during shadow window, monthly thereafter
  • High-disagreement decisions (model variance > 0.15) routed to human credit officer. — owner: Model Risk Manager, review: Continuous
  • GDPR Article 22 explanation generated and delivered with every adverse decision. — owner: Data Protection Officer, review: Quarterly audit

Human oversight measures

  • Designated credit officer per region with stop-decision authority within 4 hours of any flagged case.
  • Daily monitoring dashboard of approval/decline distributions vs. baseline.
  • Mandatory automation-bias training for credit officers, refreshed annually.
  • Weekly review of complaints lodged against AI-driven decisions.
