**Between**

- **AegisAI** — operated by [Your Name], [your address], [your email] ("AegisAI")

Date: [DD Month YYYY]

---

This letter records the parties' non-binding intent to enter a free 8-week design partnership for AegisAI's EU AI Act conformity tooling. It is not a contract for services and creates no payment obligation. The parties intend to negotiate, in good faith, a paid services agreement at the conclusion of the partnership if both wish to continue.

## 1. Scope

AegisAI will work with the Customer to produce a regulator-defensible EU AI Act Annex IV technical documentation binder, including:

- Annex III risk classification for one (1) AI system identified by the Customer (the "Pilot System")
- Compliance evaluation of the Pilot System against EU AI Act Articles 5, 9, 10, 13, 14, and 15
- Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (Article 27) for the Pilot System, where applicable
- ISO/IEC 42001 Annex A control coverage map
- Post-market monitoring (Article 72) webhook configuration

## 2. Term

8 weeks from the kickoff date of [DD Month YYYY]. Either party may terminate on 7 days' written notice for any reason.

## 3. Customer commitments

- Designate one (1) "Champion" with authority to coordinate the engagement.
- Designate one (1) "Evidence Steward" who can produce the Pilot System's existing evaluation evidence (model performance metrics, fairness tests, cybersecurity audits, instructions for use).
- Make available approximately four (4) one-hour working sessions over the term.
- Sign AegisAI's standard mutual NDA before evidence is shared.

## 4. AegisAI commitments

- Provide AegisAI software, with admin access for the Customer's nominated users, hosted in the EU region of the Customer's choice (Frankfurt, Dublin, or Amsterdam).
- Lead all working sessions personally.
- Deliver a complete Annex IV binder at week 8 in HTML and PDF.
- Deliver a written gap-and-recommendations memo at the end of week 8.

## 5. Fees

**€0.** No fee, no licence cost, no implementation cost during the 8-week term.

## 6. Reference rights

If the engagement produces a usable binder, AegisAI may reference the Customer's name, logo, and a one-paragraph summary of the engagement publicly, **only with the Customer's prior written approval of the specific wording**. The Customer may decline reference rights with no impact on this LOI.

## 7. Intellectual property

- The Customer retains all rights in its evidence and the Pilot System.
- AegisAI retains all rights in the AegisAI software and the policy packs.
- The binder, classification, and FRIA produced for the Customer are the Customer's property, exclusive to the Customer's use.

## 8. Confidentiality

The parties will execute AegisAI's mutual NDA before any evidence is exchanged. All evidence and intermediate outputs are treated as the Customer's confidential information.

## 9. No exclusivity, no obligation to continue

Neither party is committed to a paid agreement after the term. Each party may walk away at week 8 without cause and without further obligation.

This letter and any related NDA are governed by the laws of [England and Wales / Republic of Ireland / your preferred EU jurisdiction], with exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of [London / Dublin / your city].
