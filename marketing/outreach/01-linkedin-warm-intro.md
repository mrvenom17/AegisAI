# LinkedIn DM Template — Tier-1 warm-intro ask

**Use this for:** existing LinkedIn connections you've had any prior contact with — CEOs, CFOs, founders, policy makers, Big-4 partners, regulator alumni. You are *not* pitching them. You are asking advice.

**Send via:** LinkedIn manually. **Never** via a bot. Each message takes 5–8 minutes to personalise. Cap at 5 per evening.

**Reply rate to expect:** 25–40% over 7 days for Tier-1 with prior contact.

---

## Template

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Hope you're well. We connected {{specific_context}} but I haven't followed up properly.
>
> I've been heads-down for the last six months on a problem I think you'll find interesting: with the EU AI Act high-risk obligations applying on **2 Aug 2026**, every European bank with credit/underwriting/fraud AI is suddenly going to need an Annex IV technical-documentation binder, FRIA under Article 27, and ISO 42001 control coverage — produced in a way a Notified Body or supervisor will actually accept. Most institutions have 50 evals in 50 Confluence pages and call it a day; that won't survive an audit.
>
> I've built a working system that produces exactly this — signed, deterministic, regulator-defensible — and I'm running 3 free 8-week design partnerships before the deadline.
>
> Two asks if you have the bandwidth:
>
> 1. Does this match what you're seeing inside {{their_company_or_sector}}?
> 2. Is there anyone in your network — Head of Model Risk, Chief Compliance Officer, AI Governance lead at an EU-licensed bank or insurer — who'd be the right person to introduce me to for a pilot conversation?
>
> Happy to walk you through the binder we generate — sample attached.
>
> Thanks either way,
> {{your_name}}
> [link to demo binder PDF]

## Personalisation cheat-sheet

Replace `{{specific_context}}` with one of:

- "after your panel at {{event}} last year"
- "around your {{specific_topic}} post on LinkedIn"
- "via {{mutual_contact}}"
- "when {{their_company}} announced {{specific_initiative}}"
- "after I shared your piece on {{topic}} with my team"

Replace `{{their_company_or_sector}}` with their specific institution, **or** their domain (e.g. "European retail banking", "the Nordic insurance market", "the BNPL space").

If you have *no* prior context, swap the first paragraph for: *"I've been following your work on {{specific topic from their feed}} for a while — really appreciated {{specific post or interview}}."* Always concrete, never generic.

## What you do with replies

| They reply with | You reply with |
|---|---|
| "Interesting, tell me more" | Send the demo binder, the Loom, and "Happy to do a 15-min walkthrough — here's my Calendly: [link]" |
| "I don't think this is for us" | "Totally fair. Quick last ask — is there one specific person at a peer bank you'd put in front of this if you were me?" |
| "Let me think" | Wait 7 days. Reply: "Bumping this — also wanted to share {{one new specific thing}}." |
| Direct intro to a buyer | Reply within 1 hour. Thank introducer. To buyer: *short* note (4–5 lines) referencing the introducer + one-line value + Calendly. |
| No reply at all | Wait 14 days. Send one follow-up: *"{{First Name}} — bumping this once. If now isn't the right time, totally fine, just want to make sure it didn't slip."* Then leave them alone for 60 days.

## Boundaries

- Maximum 5 messages per evening.
- Personalise every single one. If you can't think of a personalisation in 60 seconds, skip them and pick a different name.
- Never send the same message to two people in the same company in the same week.
- If a contact has already replied "no" or asked you to stop, mark them in CRM as "do-not-contact" and respect it indefinitely.
