# LinkedIn Content Calendar — 12 weeks

**Cadence:** 3 posts/week. ~60 minutes/post including writing + replies.
**Goal:** become the LinkedIn voice on EU AI Act compliance for high-risk AI systems. Buyers see your name 3× before you cold-email them.

## The three post types (rotate)

### Monday — Insight post (200–400 words)
One non-obvious thing about the regulation, written like you're explaining it to a smart friend over coffee. Not a press release. Not a recap of headlines.

**Examples to draw from:**
- Why most companies misread Article 6(3)'s accessory-task override
- Why FRIA under Article 27 has to be filed *before* deployment, not after — and why most templates fail this
- What "evidence" actually means under Article 10 and why your existing data documentation isn't it
- Why your existing model-risk-management framework (SR 11-7, ECB TRIM) does about 60% of Article 9 — and where the 40% gap is
- The single most common mistake in self-attested classification questionnaires
- Why ISO 42001 certification is becoming a procurement gate even for companies with no EU exposure

**Format:** hook in line 1 ("Most banks I've talked to don't realise that..."), 3–5 short paragraphs, 1 concrete example, 1 takeaway. End with a question to the reader.

### Wednesday — Public-audit post
Pick a household-name company with public AI usage. Run their *publicly described* AI system through your Annex III classifier. Post the result.

**Format:**
- Top: "I ran {{Company}}'s publicly described {{system}} through an Annex III classifier."
- Middle: a screenshot or a clean text version of the classification + rationale
- Bottom: "If they're operating in the EU, here's what I'd flag as their highest-risk obligation: {{specific Article}}. Not legal advice. Open to the team's correction."
- Tag the company's official handle. Tag any specific named exec only if they've spoken publicly about AI governance.

**Targets to rotate:**
Klarna, Revolut, Wise, N26, Adyen, Mollie, BNP Paribas, ING, Santander, Allianz, AXA, Deutsche Bank, Nordea, KBC, Generali, Aviva, Aegon, OakNorth, Starling, Monzo, Trade Republic, Scalable Capital, BUX.

**Risk:** never accusatory, never absolute. *"Here's what I see from public information"* — they can correct you.

**Upside:** their CCO replies. Their head of AI governance DMs you. Their PR person engages and now the post is in front of every employee at the company.

### Friday — Behind-the-scenes post
Founder-craft post. Showing the work.

**Examples:**
- "This week I generated my Nth Annex IV binder. Here's what's hardest about Article 10 evidence for credit scoring..."
- "Built X this week. Here's why it took longer than expected: Article 14 has three different tiers of human-oversight that most engineers conflate..."
- "Reading the EBA's draft guidelines on model risk management for AI. Three things that surprised me..."
- "Did a 4-hour session with a {{anonymised customer}} this week. Walking out, the thing they spent most time on wasn't the binder — it was the audit log. Here's why..."

**Format:** screenshot of something concrete (always — words alone underperform). 100–250 words. End with one specific thing you're working on next.

## Hooks that work for this audience

- "Most {{audience}} don't realise that..."
- "I've now seen N {{thing}} and the same mistake comes up every time:"
- "{{Specific company}} just {{specific action}}. Here's what it tells us about {{regulatory point}}."
- "{{Number}} days to {{deadline}}. Here's the one thing {{audience}} are still ignoring:"
- "I just spent a week reading {{specific document}}. Three things stood out:"

## Hooks that don't work (skip these)

- "Excited to announce..."
- "Thrilled to share..."
- "Thoughts? 🚀"
- "What do you think?"
- Anything starting with a quote.
- Anything with more than one emoji.
- Carousel posts.

## Engagement protocol (30 min/day)

**Morning (10 min):** scroll your home feed. Comment substantively (≥ 2 sentences) on 3 posts by people in your target market. Not "great post". Add a fact, a counterpoint, a related anecdote, a question.

**Evening (20 min):** reply to every comment on your own posts. Even the bad ones. Especially the bad ones. The algorithm rewards comment depth.

## What success looks like by week 12

- 2,000–5,000 net new followers (if you start from a Tier-1 connection base)
- 30–50 inbound DMs from buyer-profile contacts
- One or two posts that go viral within the EU FinServ niche (>50k impressions) — at least one Wednesday public-audit post will, mathematically, hit
- Recognition — when you cold-email a target, ~30% reply "I've seen your posts"

## What success doesn't look like

- 100k followers in 12 weeks. That's a different game (broad-audience tactics) and not your job.
- Going viral on tech Twitter / Hacker News. You're not trying to reach engineers; you're trying to reach Heads of Model Risk.
- Becoming a "thought leader". You're trying to become the *one specific person* a Head of Compliance trusts on this *one specific topic*.
